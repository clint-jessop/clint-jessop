About Me

Clint is a student of Digital Design at Bridgerland Technical College in Logan, Utah. He is chasing the feeling that Agnes Pelton's painting "Nurture" evoked in him when he saw it, as a teenager, at the Nora Eccles Harrison Museum of Art. Shapes, abstraction, bright colors, and monoliths inspire him.
